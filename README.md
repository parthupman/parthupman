### 👋 About

I build machine learning systems and research on AI security and reliability.  
My work centers on **machine unlearning** — making models forget on demand — and extends into the
questions around it: what a model **leaks**, how it can be **attacked**, and when it can be **trusted**.

I'm an **MSc student at the University of Nottingham**, following a **B.Tech in Computer Science from BIT Mesra**.

---

### 🔬 Research Areas

**🧠 Machine Unlearning** *(core)*
Removing knowledge, concepts, and capabilities from trained models — and certifying that the removal is real, durable, and doesn't collapse what should stay. LLM unlearning, certified and decentralized removal, graph and federated unlearning, sequential editing, and concept erasure in diffusion models.

**🔐 Memorization & Privacy**
Membership inference from both sides of the table — building stronger attacks to find what a model retained, and stronger defenses against them. If a method claims a privacy property, I'd rather try to break it first.

**🛡️ Adversarial Robustness & Model Safety**
Defending models against manipulation — prompt-injection and jailbreak defense, backdoor purification, and hazardous-capability removal that holds up under adaptive attack.

**📐 Conformal Prediction & Uncertainty**
Distribution-free coverage guarantees that survive contact with reality — long-tailed label distributions, covariate shift, conformal factuality control for LLM claims, and variance-reduced calibration from small calibration sets.
