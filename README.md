### 👋 About

I build machine learning systems and publish research on AI security and reliability.  
My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning security** and **uncertainty quantification** —  
controlling what a model retains and reveals, and quantifying when it can be trusted.

---

### 🔬 Research Areas

**🧠 Unlearning & Model Editing**
Removing knowledge, concepts, and capabilities from trained models — and certifying that the removal is real, durable, and doesn't collapse what should stay. LLM unlearning, certified and decentralized removal, graph and federated unlearning, sequential editing, concept erasure in diffusion models.

**🔐 Memorization & Privacy**
Membership inference from both sides of the table — building stronger attacks to find what a model retained, and stronger defenses against them. If a method claims a privacy property, I'd rather try to break it first.

**🔏 Model Integrity & Provenance**
Backdoor purification, watermarking, fingerprinting, and attribution that survives paraphrase and fine-tuning.

**📐 Conformal Prediction & Uncertainty**
Distribution-free coverage guarantees that survive contact with reality — long-tailed label distributions, covariate shift, conformal factuality control for LLM claims, and variance-reduced calibration from small calibration sets.
